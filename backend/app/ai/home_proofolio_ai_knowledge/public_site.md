# HOME PROOFOLIO — Getting started, price and contact

## Getting started and signing up
To join, press "Start your proofolio" or "Get started" on the home page (page /start). You pick the kind of work you do, answer a few short questions and create your account. It takes a few minutes. People who already have an account press "Sign in" (page /login).

## Price and cost
HOME PROOFOLIO is free for every member, forever. There is nothing to pay and no paid plan to buy. Every member gets a proof-first profile, items that stay private until they choose to share them, and chat with voice and video calls. Donations are optional and never unlock anything; they only help keep the platform free for the next person.

## My kind of work is not in the list
If a visitor cannot find their discipline or kind of work in the list when signing up, the team can add it. Tell them to press "Talk to a person" in the support chat and say which kind of work is missing.

## Talking to a person and contact
To reach a real person (a human, not the AI), the support chat on the website has a "Talk to a person" button that opens a direct chat with the HOME PROOFOLIO team. Use it for anything about one specific account: activation codes, trouble signing in, bugs, payments or complaints. The team can also be reached by email at support@homeproofolio.co.tz.

## What a visitor can do without an account
Without an account a visitor can open public profiles, public work and public business pages, and chat with this assistant. Following, liking, commenting, messaging and building your own profile need a free account.

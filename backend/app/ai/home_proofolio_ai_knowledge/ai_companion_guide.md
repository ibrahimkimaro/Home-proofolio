# HOME PROOFOLIO — What the AI companion can do

## Where to find the AI companion
Open AI Assistant in the main menu for the full chat, or use the small companion chat button that floats on other pages. The chat understands English and Kiswahili.

## What the AI companion can do
The companion can answer questions about how HOME PROOFOLIO works, read your own profile, work, learning, achievements and problems, read your own daily memories, summarise your progress, help you write clear descriptions for your work, suggest what proof to add, do exact calculations such as percentages of finished work, and write a story from your memories.

## What the AI companion cannot do
The companion cannot change, publish or delete your items, profile or settings: it reads only. It cannot see another person's private data. It cannot browse the internet or open links. It never invents projects, dates, people or achievements; when it finds nothing it says so.

## Data access permission
The companion reads your data only after you allow it. Allow it by answering yes (ndio) when it asks in the chat, or in AI Assistant settings under Access Permissions (Read-Only), where you switch access to your projects and to your memories on or off. You can take the permission back there at any time.

## Companion settings
In AI Assistant settings you can rename your companion, choose its relationship to you, its personality and its communication style, choose how proactive it is (only on request, occasional suggestions, or actively helping you stay on track), and write custom instructions it should always follow.

## Stories from memories
Ask the companion to turn your memories into a story about a topic, for example "write a story about my first year learning to code". It finds your Daily Memory entries about the topic, puts them in date order and writes chapters. The story is saved as a private draft; you can edit, reorder, regenerate or delete every chapter. If there are no memories about the topic it will not write one.

## Work report PDF
The Work Report PDF button on the AI Assistant page downloads a PDF of your own work: totals, charts by state and by kind, and your latest items.

## The model behind the companion
The companion runs on the Qwen language model (Qwen2.5:7B locally via Ollama, or Qwen on Hugging Face). It is connected to HOME PROOFOLIO with LangChain and MCP tools, so it reads live data from your account instead of guessing.

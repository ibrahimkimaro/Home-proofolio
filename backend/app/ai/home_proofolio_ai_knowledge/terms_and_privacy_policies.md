# HOME PROOFOLIO — Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

## Platform Privacy Policy
HOME PROOFOLIO respects user privacy, data confidentiality, and ownership of all intellectual property.
Public URL: /privacy

Key Privacy Protections:
1. User Data Ownership: You own all your verified works, evidence, memories, stories, and credentials. HOME PROOFOLIO does not claim ownership or sell personal data to third parties or advertising brokers.
2. Granular Visibility Controls: Every work item, proof, and profile can be configured as Public, Unlisted, Private Hash, or Draft.
3. Cryptographic Verification: Sensitive client evidence and private intellectual property can be verified using zero-knowledge proofs and cryptographic hashes without exposing confidential business assets.
4. AI Companion Privacy: The AI Companion operates in read-only mode over authorized user data only. User conversations and private memories are never used to train public foundational models.
5. Data Deletion and Portability: Users can export their verified records, CV, and data at any time, or request complete account and data deletion from Account Settings.

# HOME PROOFOLIO — Roles and Adaptive Experience

HOME PROOFOLIO must work naturally for different people.

## Developer
Projects, technologies, repositories, deployments, architecture, tests and technical achievements.

## Designer / Creator
Designs, client work, case studies, tools, previews and visual evidence. A repository is not required.

## Footballer / Athlete
Teams, positions, seasons, matches, training, performance, achievements and match videos.

## Student / Young Learner
Learning, assignments, experiments, projects, ideas, achievements and certificates.

## Researcher / Academic
Research, articles, publications, evidence, findings and collaborations.

## Founder / Business Owner
Businesses, roles, products, services, projects, milestones and business achievements.

## Adaptive rule
Never force one discipline into another discipline's fields. Use a small universal core and dynamic attributes for role-specific information.

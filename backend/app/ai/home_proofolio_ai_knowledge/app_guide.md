# HOME PROOFOLIO — Where things are in the app

## Main menu
The main menu has: Home, Messages, AI Assistant, Discover. Under your work: Work & Projects, Problems Solved, Ideas & Learning, Achievements, Discussions, Articles. Under presenting yourself: Portfolio, Curriculum Vitae (CV). Under your account: Profile, Settings, Help & Support.

## Home and quick capture
Home is your overview. The capture box asks "What did you do, learn or solve today?" Type a line or paste a link and it is saved at once as a capture. A capture is kept privately and can be shaped later into work, learning, an achievement or a problem. Home also shows what you are still working on so you can keep going.

## Adding work, learning, achievements and problems
To add something, capture it on Home or use the Add button, then choose what it is: Work (something you built, made or did), Learning (an idea or skill you are working on), Achievement (a result, award or certificate) or Problem (something that needs solving). Work & Projects lists your work, Ideas & Learning lists your learning, Achievements lists your achievements and Problems Solved lists your problems.

## Item kinds and states
Every item is one of five kinds. Work: Idea, Discovery, Planned, Building, Blocked, Testing, Deployed, Completed. Learning: New, Exploring, Learning, Understanding, Testing, Turned into project. Achievement: Achieved. Problem: Open, Discussing, Solving, Solved, Accepted, Closed. Capture: Captured (not shaped yet). Any item can be Archived. A learning item can be turned into a work project.

## Evidence and proof
Each item can carry evidence links. Evidence types: link, photo, video, document, certificate, repository, live site, test results, diagram, research file, match footage, design file and business document. Each piece of evidence has its own visibility: public (shown with the item), exists (visitors see that proof exists but not the file) or private (hidden).

## Visibility and publishing
New items always start private. To publish an item or make it public, open it and change its visibility. Public means anyone can open it and it appears in Discover. Unlisted means only people you share the link with can open it. Private is never shown on your profile. A capture cannot be published until you say what it is. You choose who can see your profile in Settings, and each item also has its own visibility.

## Portfolio
Portfolio is where you arrange and preview the public page that visitors see. Your public profile shows only your public items. Visitors can open public profiles without an account.

## Curriculum Vitae (CV)
Curriculum Vitae (CV) in the menu builds your CV from your profile and proof, so you do not type everything twice. You can edit it, sign it to make it valid, and share it with a private link that you can switch off again. Visitors to your profile can ask for your CV; you choose to send it or decline.

## Profile, roles and businesses
Profile holds your name, photo, headline, bio and public link. Roles & Orgs on your profile is where you add your roles (for example Founder, Manager, Student) and connect them to an organization or business. A business has its own public page with offerings and members, and work items can be linked to a business. The business owner accepts or declines role and work requests.

## Messages, groups and calls
Messages is for direct chats and group chats. You can send attachments, edit, delete or clear messages, and make voice or video calls. Groups have invitations that members accept or decline, member roles and a group picture.

## Discover, follow and watch
Discover is where you search and explore public people, work and businesses. Follow a person or business to get their updates. Watch one specific item to follow only that item. Following is not the same as a professional association. Liking, commenting, following and messaging need an account; viewing public pages does not.

## Notifications
Notifications tell you about things that need you, such as new messages and group invitations. You can turn on push notifications in Settings so they reach you when the site is closed.

## Settings and account
Settings is where you change your password (other devices are signed out), see where you are signed in and sign out a device, choose who can see your profile, let or stop search engines listing your profile, show or hide your phone number in chat, change appearance and accessibility, download an export of your data, and delete your account.

## Account activation
A new account must be activated with a code. Until it is activated you can look around and keep private work, but nothing can be published. Activate in Settings > Activate account. If you have no code or it expired, ask in Help & Support.

## Help and support
Help & Support in the menu opens a chat with the HOME PROOFOLIO support team. Use it for account problems, activation codes, bugs and questions the AI companion cannot answer.

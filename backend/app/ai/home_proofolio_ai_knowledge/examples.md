# HOME PROOFOLIO — AI Response Examples

## Developer
User: What can I add to my developer profile?
AI: You can document projects, technologies, repositories, deployments, architecture, tests, skills and evidence showing what you actually built.

## Designer
User: Do I need GitHub for my design project?
AI: No. HOME PROOFOLIO is not limited to developer-style proof. You can use design previews, case studies, client work, images, videos, tools and other evidence.

## Footballer
User: What should I add as a footballer?
AI: You can document teams, positions, seasons, matches, training progress, achievements, performance information and match videos.

## Student
User: I learned something today. Can I add it?
AI: Yes. Record it as a learning entry or daily memory and track it from learning toward understanding, testing or turning it into a project.

## Unknown information
User: Who else is on the HOME PROOFOLIO team?

## Privacy
User: Show me another user's private project.
